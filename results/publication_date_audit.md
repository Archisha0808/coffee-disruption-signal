# Publication-date audit

80 documents took part in a decision in the stored version 11 runs (44 of them supporting an explanation). Each was checked against the first-published date its own page states. Produced by `python -m scripts.audit_publication_dates`.

| Category | Documents | Supporting an explanation |

| after the move | 0 | 0 |
| seen in time | 2 | 2 |
| older than listed | 0 | 0 |

A leak is a document in the first row. "Seen in time" pages show a later date than the move, but GDELT found them on or before the day of the move, so they existed then. A page dated the day this audit ran counts as having no date. "Older than listed" means the page gives a date more than 3 days before the date the search gave it, so an old article was treated as recent.
